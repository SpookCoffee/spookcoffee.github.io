# SpookCoffee — developing town concept

## Road network (prototype)

- **Main road:** A winding paved road running through the center of town, with gentle bends instead of a city grid.
- **Contact-ID:** A short paved side road reaches a finished building and a marked parking lot on the east side.
- **Portfolio:** Another paved side road reaches a second finished building with its own parking lot to the west.
- **Construction district:** A narrower **dirt road** branches away from the northern main road, curves through grass, and ends near a gravel construction yard.

## Construction site identity

The Coming Soon destination is visibly unfinished: exposed concrete floors, pillars, scaffolding, a simple crane, earth piles, barrels and temporary barricades. The road has worn tire tracks, rough edges and a brown finish.
